Method: chrF2++ per item (chrF2 for Chinese), 95% paired bootstrap; orange = sign-flip test, 5% false-discovery rate (Benjamini-Hochberg); ACL talk excerpts plus 14 abstracts. References: the talks' human transcripts and translations (German↔Chinese: both sides translated from the English); for abstracts, the authors' own English abstract, only roughly parallel. Each talk panel is 18 excerpts from 5 talks, each excerpt in two directions (36 items); the bootstrap and the sign-flip test resample whole excerpts (both directions together), and each abstract is its own unit. Local runs (S1): the same GGUFs and engine on a GTX 1080 Ti (most Bonsai records) and an RTX 5080 (most Qwen3.5 records), checked equivalent (no systematic cross-GPU shift); continuations past the 8k cap: the 27B pair on an RTX 5080 (q8_0 KV), the 2B–9B pairs on an A100 (f16 KV), Qwen3.5 0.8B on an RTX 5080 (f16 KV); originals q8_0; the 27B pair ran on an RTX 5080, q8_0 KV throughout, at a 164k context (on the GTX 1080 Ti it fits only 98k–106k).
