Each mark is the average chrF difference (character overlap with a reference text, 0–100) on the same items, Bonsai minus a plain Qwen of about the same file size (blue: the 4-bit Qwen3.5 with fewer parameters and the 2-bit Qwen3.5 with the same; at 27B, the 2-bit Qwen3.8; last, the 4-bit Qwen3.5 Bonsai derives from) or a cloud model minus Qwen3.5 9B (gray), each against Qwen run in the same mode (GLM's low effort against Qwen thinking off). Whiskers are 95% confidence intervals for that average, not the spread of individual items; one crossing zero means no reliable difference. A test is one panel in one thinking mode. At 2.4 GB, Bonsai 9B: against Qwen3.5 4B 4-bit (2.7 GB) it is ahead in 1, behind in 1 and level in 6 of 8 tests; against Qwen3.5 9B 2-bit (3.2 GB) it is ahead in all 8 tests. At 1.1 GB, Bonsai 4B: against Qwen3.5 2B 4-bit (1.3 GB) it is ahead in 5 and level in 3 of 8 tests; against Qwen3.5 4B 2-bit (1.5 GB) it is ahead in all 8 tests (faded with thinking on: most of the Qwen’s answers unfinished at 32k). At 0.5 GB, Bonsai 2B: against Qwen3.5 0.8B 4-bit (0.5 GB) it is ahead in 6 and level in 2 of 8 tests; against Qwen3.5 2B 2-bit (0.8 GB) it is ahead in all 8 tests (faded with thinking on: most of the Qwen’s answers unfinished at 32k). At 27B, Bonsai (derived from Qwen3.8 27B) against Qwen3.8 27B UD-IQ2_XXS of the same size (7.2 vs 7.3 GB): into English it is ahead in 1 and behind in 1 of 4 tests (talks −0.7 off, +0.9 on; abstracts +1.3 off, +1.4 on), out of English behind in 1 of 2 modes (−1.8 off, +1.4 on), between German and Chinese ahead in 1 and behind in 1 of 2 modes (−1.7 off, +3.3 on). Against the 4-bit Qwen3.5 of the same parameter count, the model Bonsai 2 derives from (a 2.4–2.5x larger file): into English, Bonsai 9B, 4B and 2B trail by up to 3 points; out of English and between German and Chinese, Bonsai is behind, though not always reliably. Method: chrF2++ per item (chrF2 for Chinese), 95% paired bootstrap; orange = sign-flip test, 5% false-discovery rate (Benjamini-Hochberg); ACL talk excerpts plus 14 abstracts. References: the talks' human transcripts and translations (German↔Chinese: both sides translated from the English); for abstracts, the authors' own English abstract, only roughly parallel. Each talk panel is 39 excerpts from 10 talks, each excerpt in two directions (78 items); the bootstrap and the sign-flip test resample whole excerpts (both directions together), and each abstract is its own unit. Local runs (S1): the same GGUFs and engine on a GTX 1080 Ti (most Bonsai records) and an RTX 5080 (most Qwen3.5 records), checked equivalent (no systematic cross-GPU shift); continuations past the 8k cap: the 27B pair on an RTX 5080 (q8_0 KV), the 2B–9B pairs on an A100 (f16 KV), the 2B–9B pairs and Qwen3.5 0.8B on the GTX 1080 Ti (q8_0 KV), the 2B–9B pairs and Qwen3.5 0.8B on an RTX 5080 (f16 KV); originals q8_0; the 27B pair ran on an RTX 5080, q8_0 KV throughout, at a 164k context (on the GTX 1080 Ti it fits only 98k–106k). DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 via DeepInfra (fp8), GLM 5.3 Flash via Sail Research (fp4, low-effort thinking since it can’t be turned off); Qwen rows: Q4_K_M (4-bit) and Unsloth UD-IQ2_XXS (2-bit) GGUFs; Bonsai 2 derives from Qwen3.5 of the same size (PrismML model card); tok/s: llama-bench decode, GTX 1080 Ti, short context; local thinking on: up to 32k thinking tokens (8k-capped generations continued); cloud runs use the providers' default sampling and output limits; an empty or unfinished answer scores 0.
